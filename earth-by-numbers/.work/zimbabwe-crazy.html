Stat 033/5

Sign language is one of the sixteen.

Very few constitutions anywhere give a signed language equal official standing with spoken ones.

Source: Wikipedia.

Stat 044/5

The constitution lets languages be added, never removed.

New official languages can be created by an Act of Parliament. There is no mechanism to take one off the list.

Source: Wikipedia.

Stat 055/5

Three of the sixteen do almost all the work.

Shona and Ndebele are what most people speak. English still runs government and business — a direct inheritance of colonial rule.

Source: Wikipedia.
