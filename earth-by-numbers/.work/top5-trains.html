The Orient

hotel on wheels.

Not a journey. Five rail trips where the window is the point. Opinion.

SWIPE
No. 12/6
01
Trans-Siberian
Moscow – Vladivostok

Around 9,300 km and roughly seven days across eight time zones. The longest continuous rail service in the world.

No. 23/6
02
Qinghai–Tibet
Xining – Lhasa

Crosses the Tanggula Pass above 5,000 m. The carriages supply supplementary oxygen because passengers need it.

No. 34/6
03
Belgrade – Bar
Serbia – Montenegro

435 km, 254 tunnels, 435 bridges. Mountains down to the Adriatic in a single day, for the price of a pizza.

No. 45/6
04
Bergen Line
Oslo – Bergen

About seven hours across the Hardangervidda plateau. Snow and emptiness between two very different Norwegian cities.

No. 56/6
05
TranzAlpine
Christchurch – Greymouth

Coast to coast over New Zealand's Southern Alps in under five hours, through gorges and beech forest.
