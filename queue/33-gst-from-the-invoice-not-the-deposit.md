# 33 — Your bank deposit is not your commission

**Audience:** self-employed real estate salespeople on commission. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. Useful shortly before a GST due date.
**Source guide:** guides/gst-registration.html (rules only, not framing).
**Why it works:** Inland Revenue's real estate fact sheet warns about this in
terms. Agents who file GST from their bank feed under-declare without knowing.

## Caption
Hook: `The amount that lands in your account after a settlement is not your commission, and it is the wrong number for your GST return.`

Then: where GST is added to your commission and tax is withheld before you are
paid, the deposit is smaller than the invoice. Inland Revenue's fact sheet for
the real estate industry puts the usual withholding at 20% of the GST-exclusive
amount, and it is specific about the consequence: the GST return takes your
total commission from the invoice, and if you use the figure from your bank
statement you will be under-declaring. The tax withheld has not gone anywhere.
It is credited against your income tax at the end of the year. But it is not an
expense, and it does not reduce the sales figure. File from the agency's
commission statements, not from the bank feed.
