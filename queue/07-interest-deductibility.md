# 07 — Interest is 100% deductible again, and returns still get it wrong

**Audience:** residential rental owners. **Suggested:** 2027-01-11
**Source guide:** guides/rental-property-deductions.html
**Why now:** first post of the new year, aimed at the return about to be
prepared. The phase-in is recent enough that returns on autopilot still apply
the old percentages.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY: interest deductibility on residential rental property is **100% from
  1 April 2025**, and was 80% from 1 April 2024 —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/renting-out-residential-property/residential-property-interest-rules
- VERIFY: it applies regardless of when the property was acquired or the loan
  drawn down, and that the new build distinction no longer affects the
  percentage — same page.
- VERIFY: tracing still required (borrowing traceable to buying or improving the
  rental) and apportionment still required for private use — same page.
- VERIFY: principal repayments are never deductible — IR264.
- Note the IRD rental expenses page still carries wording reading "From 1 October
  2021 until 31 March 2025, the interest limitation rules limit..." — that is the
  historic rule. Do not quote it as current.

## Caption
Hook: `Mortgage interest on a residential rental went back to 100% deductible on 1 April 2025.`

Then: the restoration ran in stages and it now applies to every residential
rental regardless of when you bought or when the loan was drawn, so the old new
build distinction no longer changes the percentage. Two conditions still bite:
the borrowing has to be traceable to buying or improving the rental, and
interest is apportioned where there is private use. Principal repayments never
were and still are not deductible. If your last return was prepared under the
old settings, it is worth a look.
