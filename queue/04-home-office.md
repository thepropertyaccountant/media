# 04 — Home office: the square metre rate against actual costs

**Audience:** contractors and sole traders. **Suggested:** 2026-12-07
**Source guide:** guides/contractor-deductions.html
**Why now:** quiet period before Christmas is when a sole trader will actually
measure a room; the claim then runs for the rest of the income year.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY: the square metre rate for the year to 31 March 2026, last known
  **$57.30**, and check whether a rate for the year to 31 March 2027 has been
  published (Inland Revenue sets it around June for the year just ended) —
  https://www.taxtechnical.ird.govt.nz/determinations/square-metre-rate
  Use the rate for the income year the post is about, and say which year it is.
- VERIFY: what the square metre rate covers and what it does **not** —
  it covers utilities only; mortgage interest, rates and rent are claimed
  separately on the business proportion of floor area. Confirm on
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/using-your-home-for-your-business
- VERIFY: the income year end is **31 March** for a standard balance date.
- If no current square metre rate can be confirmed, build the post around the
  actual-cost method and the apportionment rule instead, and drop the rate slide.

## Slides (7)
1. **dark, hook** — h-lg: `You can claim the<br>room you work in.<br>Most people claim<br>it wrong.`
   sub: `There are two methods and they are not interchangeable.`
2. **light** — kicker `Method one`. h-lg: `A set rate for<br>every square<br>metre you use.` tag: `simple`

4. **light** — kicker `Method two`. h-lg: `Actual costs,<br>apportioned by<br>floor area.` tag: `more work, often more`

7. **deep, sign-off** — CTA: `Working from home? Measure the space before the year ends.`

## Caption
Hook: `If you work from home, you are entitled to claim the space. Most people claim it wrong.`

Then: two methods. The square metre rate is a flat amount per square metre of
business-use area that covers your utilities, with mortgage interest, rates or
rent claimed separately on the same floor-area proportion. Or you apportion
actual costs by floor area for everything. The second usually gives more and
costs you a measurement and some record-keeping. Either way the test is actual
business use of the space — a desk in the corner of a bedroom is a proportion of
that room, not the room. State the verified rate and the income year it applies to.
