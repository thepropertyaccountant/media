# 47 — GST is due on the 28th. Except twice a year.

**Audience:** self-employed real estate salespeople on commission. **Status: BANK**
**Window:** 1 to 14 January (for the 15 January date). It can also run in the
last week of April, re-angled to the 7 May date. **Outside those windows, take
the next bank row instead.**
**Source guide:** guides/gst-registration.html (rules only, not framing).
**Why it works:** a deadline coming, and it lands on the same day as a
provisional tax instalment, after a spring of settlements and a December of
spending.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY the rule and both exceptions —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/filing-and-paying-gst-and-refunds
  Last known: "A GST return is due by the 28th of the month after the end of
  your taxable period." "There are 2 exceptions to this date." "The GST return
  for the taxable period ending 31 March is due by 7 May." "The GST return for
  the taxable period ending 30 November is due by 15 January." Also in the GST
  guide IR375.
- VERIFY the actual due date for the period in question on IRD's key dates —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/filing-and-paying-gst-and-refunds/filing-gst
  A due date that falls on a weekend or public holiday moves to the next
  working day. 15 January 2027 is a Friday; check the year you are in.
- VERIFY slide 4: the second provisional tax instalment for a standard 31 March
  balance date is due **15 January** —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/provisional-tax/paying-your-provisional-tax
  Not everyone has a January instalment (six-monthly GST filers have different
  provisional tax dates). Keep slide 4 worded as "for many".
- Slide 5 is observation about the commission cycle, not a tax rule.
- No rates are needed. Do not add the GST rate or a worked figure.

## Caption
Hook: `GST is due on the 28th of the month after your period ends. Except twice a year, and one of those is 15 January.`

Then: the return for the period ending 30 November is due on 15 January, and
the return for the period ending 31 March is due on 7 May. Every other period
follows the 28th. The January one deserves the attention. For many people it is
also the day a provisional tax instalment is due, so two bills arrive together.
And for anyone paid on commission, it is GST on the spring's settlements,
falling due after a month of Christmas spending. The answer is boring and it
works: move the GST out of your main account as each commission lands, because
that money was never yours.
