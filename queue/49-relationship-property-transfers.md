# 49 — Separating? The property transfer is not a bright-line sale.

**Audience:** residential rental owners. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. Handle the tone with care — the reader may be in
the middle of it.
**Source guide:** guides/bright-line-test.html
**Why it works:** relief on the transfer, and a trap on the sale that follows,
because the original purchase date comes across with the title.

## Caption
Hook: `When property passes between partners under a relationship property agreement, the transfer itself is not taxed under the bright-line test.`

Then: that is rollover relief, and it means a separation does not create a tax
bill just because a title changes hands. What it does not do is reset anything.
The person who receives the property takes on the original start date, so the
bright-line period runs from when the property was first owned in the
relationship. A sale soon after the transfer can therefore still fall inside
the two years, with tax on the profit unless the property is your main home.
In a separation the sale often follows quickly, for obvious reasons. It is
worth checking the original purchase date before a property is listed, not
after an agreement is signed.
