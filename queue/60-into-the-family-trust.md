# 60 — Moving the rental into your trust no longer restarts the clock

**Audience:** residential rental owners with, or setting up, a family trust.
**Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. Keep it a few weeks clear of post 59 and the other
bright-line posts.
**Source guide:** guides/bright-line-test.html
**Why it works:** owners still believe a transfer to their own trust starts a
new two-year period. For transfers from 1 July 2024 it need not — but the
relief has conditions that depend on what the trust deed says.

## Caption
Hook: `Transferring your rental into your family trust no longer has to restart the bright-line clock.`

Then: a transfer to a trust is a change of owner, and a change of owner is
normally what starts a new bright-line period. For transfers from 1 July 2024,
rollover relief can apply instead. The person transferring is not taxed, the
clock does not restart, and the trust is treated as having bought the property
when you did, for what you paid. The relief has conditions. Every beneficiary
of the trust has to be associated with the person transferring, and to have
been for at least two years, or be a charity. It can be used once in any
two-year period. Whether a particular trust meets that depends on who its deed
names as beneficiaries, and older deeds are often drawn very widely. Read the
deed against the conditions before the transfer, not after.
