# 20 — Fees came off your commission before you saw it. Were they claimed?

**Audience:** self-employed real estate salespeople on commission. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. February to June suits it, around return season.
Keep it a few weeks clear of post 33 (GST from the invoice).
**Source guide:** guides/contractor-deductions.html (rules only, not framing).
**Why it works:** desk, administration and franchise charges are taken out
before the money arrives, so they are invisible in a bank feed. They get
missed, or they get claimed on top of an income figure that already excludes
them.

## Caption
Hook: `If your agency takes its fees out of your commission before paying you, those fees have either never been claimed or have been claimed twice.`

Then: desk fees, administration charges and the like are costs of earning your
commission. Because they are deducted before the money reaches you, they never
appear in your bank account, and a return put together from the bank feed has
no trace of them. Whether to claim them comes down to one question: is the
income figure you are filing the commission before those fees, or after? If it
is before, the fees are an expense still to be claimed. If it is after, they
have already come out, and claiming them again counts them twice. The answer
is on your commission statements, set against the income figure Inland Revenue
holds for you. It is worth ten minutes with both in front of you.
