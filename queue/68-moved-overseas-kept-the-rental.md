# 68 — Moved overseas? The Auckland rental still files here.

**Audience:** residential rental owners living, or about to live, overseas.
**Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. Keep it a few weeks clear of post 63.
**Source guide:** none on the site yet.
**Why it works:** people who leave New Zealand assume they have left its tax
system. The rental stays behind, and so does the return.

## Caption
Hook: `If you move overseas and keep your New Zealand rental, the rent is still taxed in New Zealand.`

Then: that is true even once you have stopped being a New Zealand tax resident.
The income comes from New Zealand property, so New Zealand taxes it. If you
were non-resident for the whole year, the return is an IR3NR, and it is due on
7 July like everyone else's unless a tax agent has you on an extension. The
country you now live in may also take an interest in the rent, and a tax treaty
between the two can affect how it is taxed. The part people get wrong is the
part before they leave: nobody is collecting the records, the property manager
is emailing an address nobody reads, and the first return from overseas is two
years late. A New Zealand bank account for the rent and someone here who holds
the paperwork fixes most of it.
