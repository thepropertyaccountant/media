# 38 — Under $1,000 comes off in full. Unless you bought six at once.

**Audience:** residential rental owners. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. Leave some weeks between this and post 03

**Source guide:** guides/rental-property-deductions.html
**Why it works:** everyone has heard of the $1,000 write-off. Almost nobody has

## Caption
Hook: `Anything under $1,000 for the rental comes off in full the year you buy it. Unless it came with five more just like it.`

Then: the low-value asset rule lets you deduct the whole cost of an asset of
$1,000 or less in the year of purchase, instead of depreciating it. It is the
most used rule in a chattels schedule and the least read. The condition is that
the asset was not bought from the same supplier at the same time as other
assets with the same depreciation rate. Bought together, they are looked at
together. So one heater is a write-off, and a matching set for every room on a
single invoice is a depreciation schedule. Nothing is lost, since the cost
still comes off over the life of the assets, but the timing is different and
the return needs to show it the right way.
