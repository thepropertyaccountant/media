# 08 — Vehicle claims: kilometre rates against actual costs

**Audience:** contractors and sole traders. **Suggested:** 2027-01-18
**Source guide:** guides/contractor-deductions.html
**Why now:** a logbook started in January still covers a fair slice of the
income year, and the choice of method is made before the records exist.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY every rate used, for the correct income year. Inland Revenue publishes
  kilometre rates around June for the year just ended, so **check whether a
  newer set has been issued** —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/types-of-business-expenses/claiming-vehicle-expenses
  Last known Tier 1 / Tier 2 per kilometre:
  petrol or diesel **$1.20 / $0.37** (note: confirm whether petrol and diesel are
  still quoted separately — earlier guidance had diesel at $1.30 / $0.38),
  petrol hybrid **$0.90 / $0.24**, electric **$1.22 / $0.23**.
  Do not publish any of these without confirming them on the day.
- VERIFY: the Tier 1 / Tier 2 split — Tier 1 applies to the business portion of
  the first 14,000 km in the year, Tier 2 after that. Confirm the 14,000 figure.
- VERIFY: the logbook requirement — last known a **90 day** test period, valid
  for up to **3 years** unless use changes by more than 20%. Confirm all three.
- VERIFY: the kilometre rate method covers running costs and depreciation, so
  depreciation is not also claimed. Confirm before stating it.

## Caption
Hook: `You can claim the car. You cannot claim it twice, and most of the arguments start there.`

Then: two methods — a set rate per business kilometre, or the business
proportion of what the vehicle actually costs you. The rate method bundles
running costs and depreciation together, so you do not also depreciate the
vehicle. Both methods need a logbook, kept for a test period and then good for
a few years unless your use changes materially. The method is worth choosing
deliberately, because the records you need differ and you cannot reconstruct
them in March. Quote only rates verified at build time, and say which income
year they apply to.
