# 01 — The $60,000 GST test is a rolling 12 months

**Audience:** contractors and sole traders. **Suggested:** 2026-11-16
**Source guide:** guides/gst-registration.html
**Why now:** pre-Christmas is when a tradie's turnover spikes and quietly crosses
the line mid-year, months before they would ever look at a tax year total.

## Caption
Hook: `The $60,000 GST line is not drawn at the end of your tax year.`

Then: the threshold is turnover in any twelve-month period — so a quiet winter
and a busy summer can cross it in February while your tax year total looks
fine. It also looks forward: if you expect to pass $60,000 in the next twelve
months, you must register now. Being late does not cancel the GST on the sales
you made after you crossed; it just means you are paying it out of money you
already spent, because you never charged it to the customer. Add up the last
twelve months at the end of each quarter. It takes a minute and it is the only
version of the test that matches the rule.
