# 61 — Inland Revenue wrote a fact sheet for real estate. Read the list.

**Audience:** self-employed real estate salespeople on commission. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. February to June suits it, around return season.
Keep it a few weeks clear of posts 19, 30 and 41, which each take one item
from the same fact sheet.
**Source guide:** guides/contractor-deductions.html (rules only, not framing).
**Why it works:** the regulator has published, by name, what this exact
audience can claim. Most of them have never seen it.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY every item against IR830A "Business expenses in the real estate
  industry" (August 2026; **look for a newer edition**) —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir800---ir899/ir830a/ir830a.pdf
  linked from https://www.ird.govt.nz/topics/working-in-the-real-estate-industry
- VERIFY the three tests, last known: "you must have spent the money yourself
  and were not reimbursed"; "it must be directly related to earning your
  business income"; "you must have a record to prove it."
- VERIFY each row on slide 3 is on the fact sheet's list. Last known: it names
  "advertising & marketing", "renewal of REA licence", "ongoing training
  including the cost of seminars or conferences attended that directly relate
  to your work as a real estate salesperson/agent", "ACC levies", and "wages
  you've paid (for example to personal assistants or salespeople)". **Drop any
  row that is not there on the day.**
- VERIFY slide 6: "You can claim a 50% deduction for a business lunch/dinner as
  this is entertainment expenditure."; "You cannot claim a deduction for
  personal meal and beverage expenses."; "You cannot claim entertainment
  expenses for family or friends."
- **DO NOT STATE** that the cost of first getting licensed is not deductible.
  The fact sheet says "renewal" and "ongoing" and is silent on the initial
  qualification. Slide 5 stays with what it says.
- Wages to a spouse or partner need prior approval (post 54). If slide 3 keeps
  the wages row, the caption should not suggest paying family.

## Caption
Hook: `Inland Revenue has published a fact sheet on business expenses written specifically for the real estate industry.`

Then: it starts with three tests. You must have spent the money yourself and
not been reimbursed, the cost must be directly related to earning your income,
and you must have a record to prove it. Then it names things. Advertising and
marketing. Renewal of your REA licence. Ongoing training, including seminars
and conferences that relate directly to your work. ACC levies. Wages you pay
an assistant. A business lunch at 50%. It is just as specific about what does
not qualify: your own meals, and entertaining family or friends. None of it is
surprising, but a list from the regulator is a better checklist than memory.
Put last year's return beside it and go line by line.
