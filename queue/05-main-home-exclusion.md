# 05 — The main home exclusion to the bright-line test

**Audience:** residential rental owners. **Suggested:** 2026-12-14
**Source guide:** guides/bright-line-test.html
**Why now:** families move over summer; the "I lived in it first" assumption is
tested every January. Distinct from the posted bright-line post, which was
about when the clock starts and stops.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY: bright-line period, last known **2 years** for sales on or after
  1 July 2024 — https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/buying-and-selling/bright-line
- VERIFY: the main home exclusion as it currently stands, including how much of
  the period the property must have been your main home and how the
  predominant-use test works — Inland Revenue's main home exclusion page.
  Do not state a fraction or a percentage without confirming it on the day.
- VERIFY: whether the exclusion can be used repeatedly, and any limit on that.
- VERIFY: the position where only part of the property was the main home
  (apportionment).
- If the exclusion's mechanics cannot be confirmed precisely, narrow the post to
  the single point that "I lived there at some stage" is not the test, and cite
  only what is confirmed.

## Slides (7)
1. **dark, hook** — h-lg: `Living in it first<br>does not make it<br>your main home.`
   sub: `Not for the whole time you owned it, anyway.`
2. **light** — the exclusion exists and what it does. tag: `no bright-line tax`
3. **dark** — the condition people assume, stated plainly and corrected.
4. **light, rows** — a timeline ledger: lived in it, rented it out, sold —
   with the verdict in the right column.
5. **dark** — part of the property, or part of the period: apportionment.
6. **light** — what to do: check it before you list, not after you sign.
7. **deep, sign-off** — CTA: `Moving out and renting it out? Check the exclusion before you list.`

## Caption
Hook: `Having lived in the house does not, by itself, keep it out of the bright-line test.`

Then: explain the exclusion as verified, the predominant-use condition, what
happens when the property was your home for part of the period and a rental for
the rest, and the apportionment that follows. The practical point is the same as
always with bright-line: this is decided by dates and use you cannot change
after the fact, so it is a question for before you list.

General-information line, then hashtags.
