# 48 — The tax on last year's rent is due 7 February

**Audience:** residential rental owners. **Status: BANK**
**Window:** 15 January to 6 February. **Outside that window, take the next
bank row instead.** The dates in the draft are for the year ended 31 March
2026; if this runs in a later year, move every date forward a year.
**Source guide:** guides/provisional-tax.html
**Why it works:** a deadline coming. Owners whose rental turned a profit for
the first time, now that interest is fully deductible again and loans have
been paid down, often do not know a bill is sitting in myIR.

## Caption
Hook: `If your rental made a profit in the year to 31 March 2026, the tax on it is due on 7 February.`

Then: nothing is deducted from rent as it comes in, so the tax is settled after
the return is filed. For most people that bill falls due on 7 February the
following year, or 7 April if a tax agent has you on an extension of time. It
catches owners whose rental has only recently turned a profit, because for
years there was nothing to pay and no reason to look. Miss the date and late
payment penalties and interest both apply. And if the bill is more than $5,000,
provisional tax applies to the year you are already in, which means instalments
through the year. The amount has been sitting in myIR since the return was
filed. This week is a good time to read it.
