# 69 — The rental return for the year to 31 March is due 7 July

**Audience:** residential rental owners. **Status: BANK**
**Window:** 1 June to 6 July. **Outside that window, take the next bank row
instead.**
**Source guide:** guides/rental-property-deductions.html
**Why it works:** a deadline coming, and a second date (the tax agent's
extension) that most owners have heard of and few understand.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY the due date —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/individual-income-tax-return---ir3
  Last known: "You need to send us your completed return by 7 July unless you
  have a tax agent or an extension of time."
- VERIFY the extension —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/topics/intermediaries/extension-of-time-arrangements
  Last known: "The Commissioner can give tax agents clients an extension of
  time to file up to 31 March the following year." "We may withdraw a clients'
  extension of time for the following tax year if their return isn't filed by
  31 March."
- VERIFY whether 7 July falls on a weekend in the year in question, and use
  the date on Inland Revenue's key dates if it has moved.
- VERIFY slide 5 against the current IR3R rental schedule: it has a box for
  total rents and separate boxes for the main expenses, including agent's
  collection fees. Name only boxes that are on the form.
- VERIFY slide 2: that rental income has to be returned in an individual
  income tax return (IR3) —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/renting-out-residential-property/residential-rental-income-and-paying-tax-on-it/pay-tax-on-your-rental-income
- An IR3 guide page was read as giving "31 March 2026" for the agent date on a
  return due 7 July 2026. That looks like a typo or a misreading. Use the
  extension-of-time page's wording, and **do not put a year on the agent date**
  unless it is confirmed.
- Slide 6 is practical advice, not a stated rule.

## Caption
Hook: `If you own a rental and do your own tax, the return for the year to 31 March is due on 7 July.`

Then: rent is not taxed as it comes in, so it has to go in a return, with a
rental schedule showing the total rents and each cost on its own line. The due
date is 7 July. Clients of a tax agent can have an extension of time, up to 31
March the following year, but it is not automatic and it can be withdrawn for
the next year if a return comes in late. The return itself is rarely the slow
part. The slow part is three documents: the bank's summary of interest for the
year, the property manager's annual statement, and the insurance renewal. Ask
for those first, this week, and the rest is an afternoon.
