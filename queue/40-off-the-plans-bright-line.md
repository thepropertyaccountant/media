# 40 — Bought off the plans? Your bright-line clock started early.

**Audience:** residential rental owners and investors. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time.
**Source guide:** guides/bright-line-test.html
**Why it works:** it corrects the rule the practice's own bright-line post
taught (clock starts at title) for the one case where it does not. Mostly good
news, with the end-date sting still attached.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY: bright-line period **2 years** for sales on or after 1 July 2024 —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/buying-and-selling/when-you-need-to-pay/the-brightline-test
- VERIFY the ordinary start date: the date the property's title is transferred
  (registered) to you —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/buying-and-selling/when-you-need-to-pay/the-brightline-test/start-and-end-dates
- VERIFY the off-the-plans start date, same page and the bright-line guide
  IR1229: for property "acquired relying on the completion of a land
  development or subdivision", the start date is the "Date you entered into a
  sale and purchase agreement"; "an earlier start date for the bright-line
  period is used" —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir1200---ir1299/ir1229/bright-line-property-tax.pdf
- VERIFY the end date has not changed: the period ends when you enter into a
  binding sale and purchase agreement to sell — start-and-end-dates page.
- Slide 5 is an illustration. Label it as an example. The last row gives a
  month, not a day, on purpose: **do not state the exact day the two years
  expires** without working it through against IRD's wording.

## Caption
Hook: `If you bought off the plans, your bright-line clock started when you signed the contract, not when the title reached you.`

Then: for most purchases the two years runs from the date the title is
registered in your name. Property bought off the plans is the exception. There
the start date is the day you entered into the agreement to buy, which can be
a year or more before the building is finished. The build time counts toward
your two years, and that is in your favour. The other end has not moved. The
clock still stops on the day you sign an agreement to sell, not on settlement,
so the same rule that catches other sellers catches you. If a sale is anywhere
near the two-year mark, count from the contract date and check before you list.

General-information line, then hashtags.
