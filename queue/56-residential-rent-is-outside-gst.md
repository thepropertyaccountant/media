# 56 — GST registered? Your rental is still outside it.

**Audience:** residential rental owners who are GST registered for something
else — including self-employed salespeople who own a rental. **Status: BANK**
**Best deployed:** any time. This one reaches both halves of the audience.
**Source guide:** guides/gst-registration.html
**Why it works:** people registered for their business assume the
registration covers everything they do, and claim GST on the rental's repairs.

## Verify before building
- VERIFY both sentences, IR264 (March 2026 edition, GST section): "Renting out
  residential property long-term is exempt from GST." "You cannot claim GST on
  expenses from a residential property." —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir200---ir299/ir264/ir264.pdf
- VERIFY the web page: "There's no GST to pay on rental income from long-term
  residential renting." —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/renting-out-residential-property/gst-and-renting-out-residential-property
- VERIFY slide 6: "renting out short-stay accommodation is a taxable activity" —
  https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/renting-out-residential-property/gst-and-renting-out-residential-property/gst-and-your-short-stay-rental-income
- **DO NOT STATE** what "long-term" means in days or weeks unless Inland
  Revenue's page gives a figure on the day.
- **DO NOT** bring in commercial rent. The audience owns houses.
- Slide 4 is observation about how the mistake happens, not a stated rule.

## Caption
Hook: `Being GST registered for your business does not bring your rental property into GST.`

Then: renting out residential property long-term is exempt from GST. There is
no GST on the rent, and for the same reason there is no GST to claim back on
the rental's costs: not the repairs, not the insurance, not the new oven. The
mistake happens when one person has a GST-registered business and a rental,
and the receipts for both end up in the same place. The plumber's invoice for
the rental goes into the GST return with everything else, and a refund is
claimed that was never available. It is an easy thing to get wrong and an easy
thing for Inland Revenue to find. Keep the rental's paperwork apart from the
business. Short-stay letting is a different activity with different rules.
