# 21 — Commission clawed back after you have filed

**Audience:** self-employed real estate salespeople on commission.
**Status: HELD — needs Felix. Do not build.**
**Hold until:** Felix confirms the tax treatment in writing (see below).
**Source guide:** none.
**Why it would work:** deals fall over after the commission has been paid and
the return filed. Nobody knows which year the repayment belongs in.

## Why this is held
Searches of ird.govt.nz and taxtechnical.ird.govt.nz on 13 November 2026 found
**no Inland Revenue guidance** on repaying income returned in an earlier year
in a self-employed context. IR830 and IR830A do not mention clawbacks. The only
general statement found was in IR320: "If you find you've made a mistake on a
tax return you've sent in, please contact us as soon as possible" — which is
about errors, and a clawback is not an error in the original return.

BUILD.md does not allow a rule to be published that cannot be confirmed against
an Inland Revenue source. The whole value of this post is the answer to one
question, and that answer could not be sourced:

> Is a repaid commission (a) deducted in the year it is repaid, or (b) dealt
> with by amending the return for the year it was first received?
